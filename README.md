# 💜 FocusFlow – Study Time Tracker

FocusFlow is a personal productivity website designed to help students track their study time, manage study sessions, and build consistent study habits.

## ✨ Features

- Start Day and End Day tracking
- Check In and Check Out for study sessions
- Live study-time counter
- Pomodoro timer
- Custom countdown timer
- Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly study statistics
- Study history and progress chart
- Dark purple theme
- Automatic data saving using Local Storage

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Local Storage

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open FocusFlow in your browser.
2. Click **Start Day** when you begin your day.
3. Click **Check In** when you start studying.
4. Click **Check Out** when you take a break.
5. Click **End Day** before finishing your day.
6. View your study statistics and progress.

## 💡 Project Goal

To help students understand their study habits, track their progress, and stay consistent with their learning goals.
